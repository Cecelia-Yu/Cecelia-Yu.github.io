Hi, this is Cecelia!!

I’m an enthusiastic learner, while my core motivation is

**How do human adapt to tools?**

By tools, I mean everything created by human to describe or control the world, from symbolics to AI.
By adapt, I mean more than learning to use, but also how tools and human reshape each other in such process.

This has many sub-questions and also requires building up apparatus, and each leads to new sub-questions, e.g.

  • What’s the border of brain decoding?
  • How to push Cog-Sci to the wild world?
  • How to connect Cog-Sci with more data-driven methods?
